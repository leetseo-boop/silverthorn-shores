# Remove half-day rental wording

## Goal
Make it clear throughout the website that Silverthorn does not offer half-day or hourly small-boat rentals.

## Changes
1. Replace the homepage Small Boat Rentals image caption “Half or full day” with accurate full-day rental wording.
2. Correct the Small Boats rental-hours FAQ by removing the claim that half-day rentals are available.
3. Add a direct FAQ: “Do you offer half-day or hourly boat rentals?” Answer: no; rentals are full-day or multi-day only.
4. Add the same answer to the main FAQ page so visitors can find it from either location.
5. Keep Thorn aligned with the existing rule that all boat rentals are full-day or multi-day, with no half-day or hourly rentals.
6. Search customer-facing site copy again after the edits and verify the homepage and Small Boats FAQ on desktop and mobile.

## Scope
- Preserve legitimate phrases such as “a full day on the water” and unrelated hourly Shasta Dam tours.
- Do not change rental prices, hours, booking links, or other policies.
